## Responsiveness and Accessibility  
| Device | Breakpoint |Behavior |
|---|---|---|
| Desktop | `≥ 1024px` | Expanded sidebar, full tables, and multi-column dashboards |
| Tablet | `768px – 1023px` | Collapsed sidebar, condensed grids, and adapted tables |
| Mobile | `< 768px` | Compact navigation, vertical content, and single-column forms |
